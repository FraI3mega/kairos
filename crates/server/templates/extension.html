What it does

When you switch tabs, the extension checks if it's a project running on your computer or something else. It tells the Kairos app on your computer, and that's it.

The time you spend testing your app on localhost then counts as focus time, next to your editor and terminal time.

What stays private

Nothing about the page itself leaves your computer. No addresses, no page titles, no text you type or read. The extension only talks to the Kairos app on your computer, and it doesn't look inside private windows.

It needs the tabs permission only to read the address locally, pick a category and forget it.

Before you install

Kairos needs to be installed and running first. Look for the pixel clock in your tray or menu bar. If you don't have it yet, the steps are on your dashboard.
