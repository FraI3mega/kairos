How it works

Just do the first 2 steps and Kairos starts working automatically!

What Kairos sends

Just enough to add up your hours. Your code and prompts stay on your computer.

Sent to your dashboard

  • When you worked and for how long
  • How many words you typed
  • The project name, from your git remote or folder
  • Which files in the project you worked on or committed
  • Whether you were in an editor, a terminal, a browser or an AI app

Stays on your computer

  • Your code
  • Your prompts and the agent's replies
  • Window titles, tab addresses and where your folders are
  • Your screen. Kairos doesn't take screenshots.

Questions

What counts as focus time?

Writing prompts, reading replies, editing code and testing your app. It counts in your AI tools, editor and terminal. When the agent works while you're away, that's agent time instead.

Can I pause it?

Yes. The clock icon in your tray or menu bar pauses Kairos for 30 minutes, an hour or until tomorrow, and you can resume whenever you like. You can also run kairos pause.

Can I keep a folder private?

Yes. Run kairos exclude in that folder and Kairos won't record anything from it, not even the time. kairos exclude --list shows what you've excluded.

How do I remove it?

Run kairos uninstall.

Is it open source?

Yes, under AGPL-3.0, so you can read exactly what it does on your computer.
